

The project aims to develop a **smart seismic-resilient building prototype** that combines structural monitoring, sensor technology, artificial intelligence, and automated waste-management to improve safety and disaster response during and after earthquakes.

The physical prototype will represent a building equipped with **seismic/ vibration sensors** that continuously monitor ground or structural vibrations. The collected sensor data will be processed by a microcontroller and analysed using an **AI-based system** to identify abnormal vibration patterns and classify the severity of seismic activity. Based on the detected conditions, the system can generate appropriate alerts and provide information about the building's structural response.

The project will also incorporate a **smart waste-management subsystem** designed for post-disaster situations. Earthquakes and other disasters can generate large quantities of debris and disrupt normal waste collection, making rapid identification and management of waste important for safe recovery. The system will therefore demonstrate how AI-assisted monitoring of waste containers can support the segregation, monitoring, or prioritisation of waste generated during a disaster scenario.

The prototype will integrate the contributions of multiple engineering disciplines. **Civil Engineering** will focus on the building model, structural behaviour, and seismic considerations; **EEE** will handle sensors, circuits, power supply, and electronic interfacing; **CSE** will contribute to programming, data acquisition, communication, and system integration; and **AI** will be used for analysing sensor and waste-related data and developing the intelligent decision-making component.

The final outcome will be a **working interdisciplinary prototype** demonstrating how real-time sensing, structural monitoring, AI-based analysis, and smart waste-management can be combined into a single disaster-management system. The project is intended as a proof-of-concept prototype rather than a full-scale earthquake prediction or structural safety system.
